# Yellow Taxi Goes Vroom

## Where is the options page?

The player options page for this game contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file. Player
options page link: [Yellow Taxi Goes Vroom Player Options Page](../player-options).

## What does randomization do to this game?
All Gears are randomized into the pool. By default, the Flip-O-Will is split into several move items which are needed to progress through the game. Safes, Chests, Coin Bags, Coins, Checkpoints, Cheeses, and more can be locations depending on settings.

## What is the goal of Yellow Taxi Goes Vroom when randomized?
There are currently 3 different goal options. Defeating Bomboss in Bombeach, defeating Alien Mosk in Tosla Offices, or helping Maurizio in Maurizio's City

## Which items can be in another player's world?
Depending on settings, items other players can send you include Gears, the blueprints for the Golden Spring and Propeller, Moves, the Orange Switch, Morio's Password, and more.

## What does another world's item look like in Yellow Taxi Goes Vroom?
Randomized locations that have not yet been checked are displayed in color, while ones that have already been checked or are not randomized appear in greyscale. Otherwise they are visually unchanged.
 
## When the player receives an item, what happens?
When you receive an item, a message will pop up to inform you where you received the item from, and which one it is. Items received will apply their changes instantly as applicable.
